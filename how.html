How Twin Works

A clearer path from roommate search to shared space.

Four steps to a better roommate search
StepWhat you doWhy it matters
1. Build your preferencesAdd budget, location, routine, and lifestyle priorities.Matches begin with the things that affect everyday living.
2. Browse matchesCompare profiles with similar housing goals.You spend less time sorting through obviously incompatible options.
3. ConnectTalk about schedules, guests, chores, bills, and expectations.A good conversation can reveal whether a match works beyond a profile.
4. Decide togetherMeet safely and verify housing details before making commitments.Roommate compatibility and housing legitimacy both matter.

Before you commit

Twin encourages students to meet prospective roommates, verify the housing and lease details, discuss how expenses will be divided, and avoid sending money before they understand what they are agreeing to.
