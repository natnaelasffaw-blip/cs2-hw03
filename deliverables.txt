Questions
What was the most satisfying part of this assignment?
The most satisfying part was turning the original Twin idea into a complete website that feels consistent across multiple pages. I especially liked seeing the navigation, roommate profiles, and responsive layout come together into something that looks like a real service rather than separate HTML exercises.

What was the most challenging part of this assignment?
The most challenging part was keeping the layout consistent while also meeting many different technical requirements. I had to think about how the external stylesheet, internal styles, form, table, images, hover effects, and mobile layout could all fit naturally into the same site instead of looking like features added only for the assignment.
